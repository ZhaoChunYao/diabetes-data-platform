Measurement Frequencies & Intervals

  • CGM (Continuous Glucose Monitoring): Readings every 5-15 minutes depending on device and settings
  • Heart Rate (HR): Approximately every 1 minute during continuous monitoring; may vary with activity level
  • Oxygen Saturation (SpO2): Variable intervals, typically during sleep or on-demand measurements; not continuously monitored
  • Sleep Duration: Session-based measurements captured per sleep session (typically 1 session per night)
  • Stress Level: Approximately every 1 minute during periods of wear; calculated from heart rate variability
  • Physical Activity (Steps): Interval-based tracking; step counts aggregated over measurement periods (typically 1-5 minute intervals)
